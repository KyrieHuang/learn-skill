## About me
Sam (fictional) runs a two-person bakery. Comfortable with a till, a spreadsheet and supplier
invoices; no finance or computer-science training. Recently started using an AI chatbot to
draft menus and answer supplier emails, and reads about interest rates because the bakery has
a variable-rate loan.

## How to teach me
Start from something that happens in the bakery, then the idea. Kitchen and shop analogies.
Short sessions, ten minutes per concept at most. Spell out acronyms.
