# Butter prices and menu costing

Butter is up 30% year on year. The magazine walks through costing a croissant: ingredients,
labor per batch, energy, and packaging, then shows how gross margin falls from 68% to 61% if the
price stays the same. Its survey says 7 in 10 customers noticed smaller portions, but only 3 in
10 noticed a 5% price rise.
