# Central bank holds rates, signals cuts later in the year

The (fictional) central bank kept its policy rate at 4.0% for a third meeting. Inflation fell to
2.8% but services prices are still rising faster than goods. The statement said cuts are
possible "once inflation is clearly on track", and markets now expect two cuts by December.

Small-business lenders said variable-rate loan repayments will stay flat until the first cut.
